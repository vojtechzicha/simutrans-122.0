# Platforms: a road change without a stop tile coming or going (rails or roads laid next to a stop) changes its
# platforms; the slow sweep (platforms_t::step) brings the numbers up to date. Layout tests/layouts/platforms.inc:
# the bay at 30,37 is a dead end (one stand). Expect: numbered 7, it keeps 7 once the road goes on north (two
# stands, the number on one of them), and the stop keeps one number, not two.
include ../layouts/platforms.inc
platformnumber 30 37 - 7
expect_platform 30 37 - 7
waydesc Road_050
track 30 37 30 33
run 5 0
platforms 30 37
expect_platform 30 37 N 7
expect_platform 30 37 S -
save platforms_road_change.sve
load platforms_road_change.sve
expect_platform 30 37 N 7
expect_platform 30 37 S -
